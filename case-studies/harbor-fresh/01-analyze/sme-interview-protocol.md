# SME Interview Protocol

**Interviewee:** senior engagement manager who reviews analysts' memos
**Length:** 45 minutes (video call, recorded with consent)
**Pre-read sent 2 days ahead:** project brief, draft learning objectives, this protocol

## Goals
1. Confirm the performance gap and its causes.
2. Collect real (anonymized) examples of assumption errors for practice cases.
3. Agree how the SME will take part in reviews: rounds, turnaround and sign-off.

## Agenda and questions

**1. Context (5 min)**
- What happens to a memo between an analyst's draft and the client?
- Where in that process do assumption errors usually surface?

**2. The performance gap (10 min)**
- Walk me through a recent memo you sent back. What was the unstated assumption?
- What does a strong analyst do differently before writing a recommendation?
- Which errors cost the most: rework time, client credibility, or wrong decisions?

**3. Causes (10 min)**
- Do analysts lack the method, or do they know it but skip it under time pressure?
- What in the templates, tools or deadlines makes the check easy to skip?
- How do you currently give feedback on these errors?

**4. Content and examples (10 min)**
- Can you share 2–3 anonymized memo extracts we could adapt into practice cases?
- Which industries or situations would feel most realistic to this audience?
- Is there a phrase or rule your best analysts use that we should teach directly?

**5. Validation and logistics (10 min)**
- Here are the draft objectives. Would meeting them fix what you see? What's missing?
- What would convince you, 60 days after launch, that it worked?
- Confirm the review plan (below).

## Agreements to confirm before closing
- [ ] Two review rounds: design document, then the built module
- [ ] Turnaround of 3 business days per round
- [ ] SME responsible for content accuracy; ID Lead accountable for instructional quality (see RACI)
- [ ] One named sponsor for final sign-off
- [ ] Date for the next checkpoint

## After the interview
Send a written summary within 24 hours covering findings, the agreed schedule and open questions, and ask the SME to confirm it.
