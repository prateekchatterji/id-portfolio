# Project Brief: *Mind the Gap*

> **Sample project.** The client is hypothetical. Figures marked *illustrative* show how the brief would be written; they are not real data.

## Client and sponsor
A global professional services firm ("the Firm"). The sponsor is the Head of Analyst Development; the SME is a senior engagement manager who reviews analysts' work.

## Business problem
Engagement managers report that new analysts' recommendation memos are often sent back because a conclusion rests on an unstated, untested assumption. For example, a memo credits a client's training program for a cost reduction without checking whether the program was actually used. *(Illustrative: about 3 in 10 first-draft memos are returned for this reason, costing a manager 1–2 hours of rework each.)*

## Performance goal
Before submitting a recommendation, analysts identify the assumptions their conclusion depends on and either test them or flag them for the client.

## Proposed solution
- A 15–20 minute self-paced module (Articulate Rise), built around one worked business case (*Harbor Fresh*) and practice with feedback.
- A one-page **Assumption Check** job aid for use on live work.
- An optional 2–3 minute software simulation (Captivate) showing how to log assumptions in the team's error-log template.

## Audience
Analysts in their first two years, across several countries (see the needs and audience analysis).

## Success measures (Kirkpatrick)

| Level | Measure | Target |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Reaction | Post-module rating of relevance | ≥ 4.0 / 5 |
| 2 Learning | Score on the scenario assessment | ≥ 80% |
| 3 Behavior | Memos include an explicit assumptions section (manager spot-check at 60 days) | ≥ 70% of sampled memos |
| 4 Results | Memos returned for untested assumptions | Down by a third within two quarters *(illustrative)* |

## Estimate
- Output: about 0.3 hours of learning.
- **Live engagement estimate:** 40–50 effort hours (analysis 6, design 12, build 14, reviews and QA 8, pilot and revisions 6).
- **This sample:** about 12 hours, one person performing all roles (see the RACI).

## Constraints and assumptions
- Learners have 20-minute windows between client work; the module must work on laptops and phones.
- English is the working language but a second language for many learners.
- The SME is available for one 45-minute interview and two review rounds with a 3-business-day turnaround.

## Out of scope
Translation, LMS administration, and a full critical-thinking curriculum (this is one module).
