# Design Rationale: *Mind the Gap*

**What it is:** a 20-minute Articulate Rise module that teaches early-career analysts to find and test the hidden assumptions in business recommendations. *Sample project for a hypothetical client.*

## Audience
Analysts in their first two years at a global professional services firm, working across several countries in English, often as a second language. They understand business cases but have no routine for checking an argument's unstated links. They learn in 20-minute windows between client work, on laptops and phones. [Full profile](01-analyze/needs-audience-analysis.md#4-audience-profile)

## Needs analysis in brief
- **Gap:** memos come back because a conclusion rests on an untested assumption.
- **Root cause:** mainly a skill gap. Analysts treat evidence that *helps* a conclusion as evidence the conclusion *needs*. There is also an environmental cause: the memo template never asks for assumptions.
- **Solution:** training plus a one-page job aid, and a recommended template change. Training alone would under-deliver.
- **Evidence:** the author's teaching experience, and a Round 1 item tryout with five testers. It confirmed the core error and showed that less-trained testers missed the links the text never states, which changed the design. [Round 1 results](05-evaluate/pilot-results.md)

## Accessibility and localization
- **Built to WCAG 2.1 AA:** keyboard-operable interactions, AA color contrast, alt text, meaning never carried by color alone, tested with a screen reader.
- **Plain English for second-language readers:** short sentences, one idea per screen, no idioms on screen. The title's idiom is explained on the first screen.
- **Ready for translation:** text kept out of images; culturally neutral cases (retail, logistics, banking); percentages, not currency.
- **No audio,** so nothing depends on sound and updates stay quick.
