# Learning Objectives Map

## Terminal objective
Given an unfamiliar business recommendation, the learner will identify every assumption the conclusion depends on and reject statements that only strengthen it, scoring at least **80%** on the final scenario assessment.

## Enabling objectives

| ID | By the end, the learner will be able to… | Level (Bloom) | Why it's here |
|---|---|---|---|
| EO1 | Identify the conclusion and the evidence in a business recommendation | Understand | Prerequisite for every later step |
| EO2 | Map the argument as a logic chain, **stating every link, including links the text leaves unwritten** | Apply / Analyze | Round 1: novices found the visible link (F) but missed the hidden ones (D, H) — R-06 |
| EO3 | Apply the negation test, negating minimally, to decide whether a statement is required | Apply | Core method from the source content |
| EO4 | Distinguish statements an argument *needs* from statements that only *help* it, by comparing negations ("race the negations") | Analyze / Evaluate | The core error in the needs analysis; confirmed in Round 1 (3 of 5 testers) |
| EO5 | Recognize three traps: reaction data offered as proof of impact, evidence from a different population, and claims beyond the conclusion | Analyze | Round 1: B, C and G each attracted a tester; B (course ratings as proof) — R-07 |
| EO6 | Choose the stakeholder question that best tests an argument's weakest link | Evaluate | Moves the skill from answering questions to doing the job |

## Alignment: objectives → content → practice → assessment

| ID | Content (storyboard screen) | Practice | Final assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| EO1 | S03 Three parts of an argument | S03 labeled graphic | Embedded in every case |
| EO2 | S04 Build the chain | S04 predict-the-next-link; S09 practice case | Items 1, 4 (multiple response) |
| EO3 | S06 The negation test | S06 flashcards | Items 1, 4 |
| EO4 | S07 Race the negations | S07 scenario; S09 practice case | Items 2, 5 (which statement only strengthens) |
| EO5 | S05 Pass 1 sort; callout on reaction data | S05 sorting activity; S09 | Items 1, 2, 4, 5 (traps as distractors) |
| EO6 | S10 Your stakeholder question | S10 | Items 3, 6 |

Every objective is practiced at least once before it is assessed, and every assessment item traces back to an objective.

## Link to the business goal
EO6 is the bridge to Level 3 behavior: an analyst who can name the weakest link and the question that tests it is doing, in the module, what the memo template's new "assumptions" field will ask for on the job (see the needs analysis).
